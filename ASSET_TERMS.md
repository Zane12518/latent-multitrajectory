# Published research assets

Public distribution of the accompanying inference checkpoints, full continuation states, project data and saved metrics has been approved by the rights holder. The source-code MIT license does not automatically apply to these non-code assets; no separate downstream license is granted in this release. Third-party components retain their original terms.

The project-generated Maze10 inputs and references have audited exact split identities and reference validity. The original complete generator environment and symmetry-independent split construction have not been reconstructed. This is not an official Maze benchmark or an independently held-out confirmation set.

The v0.2 reports and statistics retain this existing non-code status. No new downstream license is granted. See [the object-by-object rights inventory](docs/rights.md). SATLIB source instances and unreviewed historical models are not redistributed by this update.

## v0.3 GRU assets

The rights holder has explicitly confirmed authority and approved public distribution of the 24 custom GRU tensor-only inference weights and four per-question numeric statistics files listed in [the GRU publication scope](docs/gru/publication.md). These files retain the existing non-code status above; no new downstream modification, training reuse or redistribution license is granted. The source-code MIT license does not extend to them.

The release does not include raw SATLIB instances, project full training/selection datasets, original GRU optimizer/RNG checkpoints or the 127 historical Mixer/query-only weights. Inference weights are not full-state training checkpoints.
